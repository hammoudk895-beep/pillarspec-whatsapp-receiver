Privacy notice

How this informational website handles enquiry-related information.

Last updated: 27 October 2026.

Who operates this site

This site is presented by PillarSpec SARL. For website-related privacy enquiries, use the contact email shown below.

Information handled by the website

The website is a static information site. It has no account registration, server-submitted enquiry form, advertising scripts or analytics tags in the published source. It does not deliberately set tracking cookies. Its hosting provider may process basic technical request data, such as IP address, requested page, browser information and access timestamps, to serve the website, provide security and maintain service logs.

External communication

The optional enquiry builder composes a draft locally in your browser; it does not submit information to PillarSpec or a website server. Choosing the WhatsApp route opens a service operated by WhatsApp/Meta; choosing the email route opens your email application. You must press Send yourself. Messages and any personal information you send through those services are processed under their applicable practices as well as any business correspondence arrangements. Avoid sending sensitive documents before the scope and secure channel have been agreed.

Purposes and retention

Technical hosting data is processed for website operation and security. Correspondence is used to evaluate and respond to enquiries and to maintain related business records. Applicable retention periods for hosting logs and business correspondence depend on provider settings and business requirements and can be clarified through the contact address.

Your questions

For access, correction or privacy enquiries relating to information you have sent to PillarSpec, contact KH@Pillarspec.com. Requests will be reviewed in accordance with applicable law.

This notice describes the present informational website. It should be updated if server-submitted forms, analytics, CRM integrations or other data-processing services are added.
