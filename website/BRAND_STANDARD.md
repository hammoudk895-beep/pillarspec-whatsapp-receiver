# PillarSpec Brand Standard

Approved 30 October 2026. This is the current color and expression standard for PillarSpec SARL. It supersedes the earlier green and gold visual direction for current PillarSpec materials.

## Brand character

PillarSpec expresses earned independence and enduring professional standards. Its identity should feel maritime, technically precise, composed and accountable. Established workmanship and a clear independent voice guide the design.

Approved brand expression: **A name earned. A legacy upheld.**

Supporting positioning: **Independent vision. Enduring standards.**

The existing operational descriptor and service wording remain applicable. The expression does not establish a founding date, corporate succession, accreditation or affiliation.

## Approved colors

| Color | HEX | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deep navy | #0B1F33 | 11, 31, 51 | Primary fields, headings and body text |
| Steel grey | #8795A1 | 135, 149, 161 | Secondary graphics and details on navy |
| Heritage copper | #B78058 | 183, 128, 88 | Restrained highlights, rules, logo accents and buttons |
| Salt white | #EEF2F4 | 238, 242, 244 | Light surfaces and text on navy |

Navy should lead the identity. Steel supports its technical character. Copper is a selective accent. Salt white supplies contrast and space. Use solid colors for production masters; photographic lighting may introduce natural tonal variation.

## Readability

Use navy text on salt white or copper. Use salt white or copper text on navy. Steel and copper are unsuitable for small text on salt white; use navy or the documented darker supporting tones. Preserve meaningful plus and minus signs, units, labels and directional arrows in market updates. A palette change must not alter commercial data.

## Typography and layout

Retain the existing logo shape. Use clean, precise sans serif typography for service names, figures, tables and operational copy. A restrained serif can support the approved heritage expression in brand presentations. Keep layouts spacious, align numbers carefully and use fine rules. Photographs should show authentic maritime, terminal and inspection contexts.

## Applications

Website pages, navigation, forms, favicons and shared previews use the same tokens. Catalogue graphics use navy overlays, salt white copy, steel supporting elements and copper emphasis. Social graphics preserve their data, dates, language and labels while adopting the new colors. Email signatures and business documents use navy text with a small copper accent. Existing legal and operational wording stays intact during a color-only change.

## Voice

Use calm, specific language. Define scope and responsibilities, state what can be supported, and make commitments only when confirmed. The brand earns confidence through accurate details, consistent follow-through and careful handling of business relationships.

## Maintenance

Use the accompanying JSON and CSS tokens as production references. Current working material should adopt this standard. Dated source archives and past exploration concepts remain historical records. Account graphics and installed signatures require verification in their respective applications after installation.
